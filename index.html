Academic & Research Profile

Devanand Thanissery

I am Devanand (ദേവാനന്ദ് in Malayalam), an Associate Professor in the School of Engineering & Technology at Vidyashilp University, Bengaluru. My current work is in the area of Black-Box Data-Free Knowledge Distillation.

My research explores security vulnerabilities in deep neural networks (DNNs), black-box data-free knowledge distillation (DFKD), and various aspects of adversarial robustness in Deep Learning context. Our group recently arxived ZOPGA-KD, a generator-free DFKD method that uses only a teacher model’s output logits to synthesize training data and train a student model—without requiring access to the teacher’s architecture, parameters, gradients, or original training data.

Before joining Vidyashilp University, I was a Postdoctoral Researcher at the Centre for Brain Research (CBR), IISc, Bengaluru, where I applied signal processing and machine learning techniques to EEG data to study Alzheimer’s disease. Earlier, as a Postdoctoral Fellow at Ben-Gurion University of the Negev, Israel, I used machine learning to investigate the “language” of facial expressions.

I completed my Ph.D. in Computational Biophysics at the Institute of Mathematical Sciences (IMSc), Chennai, under the supervision of Dr. Satyavani Vemparala. IMSc is a research institute under the Department of Atomic Energy, Government of India. During my doctoral work, I used atomistic and coarse-grained molecular dynamics simulations to study biomolecules such as proteins and lipids.

Prior to my doctoral studies, I earned a B.Tech in Electronics and Communication Engineering from the University of Calicut and an M.Tech in Electronics Design and Technology from the National Institute of Technology, Calicut.

My broader interests include computational neuroscience, artificial intelligence, information theory, signal processing, embedded systems, and complex systems. Please feel free to get in touch to discuss my work or potential collaborations. For further details, view my CV below.

News

New preprint 31 October 2026

ZOPGA-KD: Zeroth Order PGA Guided Data Free Knowledge Distillation

Tuhin Subhra Mukherjee, Tiya Rose Pulikunnel, Vaidhav Adit, Devanand Thanissery, and S.H. Shabbeer Basha

This preprint introduces a generator-free, black-box DFKD method that synthesizes training samples using projected gradient ascent and zeroth-order gradient estimation. The method requires only the teacher’s output logits and achieves performance comparable to approaches that access teacher parameters.

Research Group

Our research group at the School of Engineering & Technology, Vidyashilp University, focuses on model compression, pruning, data-free knowledge distillation (DFKD), black-box DFKD, and adversarial robustness in deep learning context. We work on distilling efficient computer vision models without requiring access to its original training data or the internal parameters.
